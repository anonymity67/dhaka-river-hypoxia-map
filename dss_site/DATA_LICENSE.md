# Licence for the files in `data/`

`data/rivers.geojson` and `data/stations.json` are derived data:

- Model-predicted probabilities of hypoxia per river line and season, and each station's share of hypoxic samples, computed from the dataset below.
- River line geometries and names from OpenStreetMap.

## Source of the water-quality data

Sinha, P., Debnath, N., & Bhattacharjee, P. (2026). *Water Quality and Hydroclimatic Dataset for Rivers and Streams of Dhaka, Bangladesh* (Version 1) [Data set]. Mendeley Data. https://doi.org/10.17632/xftpv7jvx3.1

Licence of the source dataset: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), as stated on its Mendeley Data page.

## Licence of the derived values

The derived values in `data/` are shared under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). If you reuse them, credit the source dataset above and this repository, and say that the values were modified (aggregated and modelled).
