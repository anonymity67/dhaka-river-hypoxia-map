How to read this map

Each line is an OpenStreetMap waterway within 5 km of a monitoring station, coloured by the model's average predicted probability that oxygen falls below 2 mg/L in the chosen season. Click a line for its value. Values between stations are interpolated by a spatial model, so they are less certain than values at a station, and the model does not know about unmapped drains or discharges.

Method

Bayesian Bernoulli model fitted by INLA-SPDE: season effect, year effect and a Matérn spatial field (range about 26 km). Predictions are for a typical year. Leave-one-station-out AUC is 0.91, meaning the model ranks risk well at stations it has not seen. Group "Balu system" combines the Balu with the Begunbari, Pagar and Bashundhara canals.

Limits

Hypoxia here is a habitat condition (dissolved oxygen below 2 mg/L), not a measurement of fish or other life. Sampling was about once per season, so short dips are not captured. Distances in the model are straight-line, not along the river. This page is research output, not a regulatory assessment, and it should not be used for safety decisions about water use.
